1. Agreement Overview

1.1 Purpose: This Agreement establishes the commercial and legal relationship between TaxRock and Customer with respect to Customer’s use of the TaxRock platform and related services (“Services”).

1.2 Pricing: Subscription fees are set forth in Section 6 of this Agreement and in the Pricing Proposal incorporated into this Agreement, and consist of a flat monthly fee scoped to Customer’s practice.

1.3 Terms of Service: All access to the Services by Customer’s staff and Customer’s clients is subject to the TaxRock Terms of Service (“TOS”), which everyone must accept upon first login and controls all platform-level rights and obligations and may be updated by TaxRock from time to time.

1.4 Order of Precedence: If there is any conflict between this Agreement and the TOS, this Agreement controls as between TaxRock and Customer. In the event of a conflict, the Pricing Proposal controls with respect to the Subscription Fee, included capacity, and other expressly stated commercial terms. This Agreement controls in all other respects. The TOS governs individual end-users’ and Monitored Clients’ use of the platform.

2. Definitions

2.1 “Authorized Users”: Employees, contractors, or staff of Customer who are permitted by the Customer to access the Services. Authorized Users are unlimited and are not a basis for fees.

2.2 “Seats”: A single permitted login and access right for one Authorized User. Seats govern platform access only and are not the basis for subscription fees.

2.3 “Monitored Clients”: Individuals or business entities who are clients of Customer and who are provisioned within Customer’s account through the Services. Monitored Clients are clients whose IRS account information, transcripts, notices, or other tax-related data is retrieved, processed, or monitored through the Services. Monitored Clients represent the Customer’s client relationship and may be associated with one or more Monitored Taxpayers for purposes of the Services. Monitored Clients are not customers of TaxRock.

2.4 “Monitored Taxpayers”: Individual taxpayers, including individuals or business entities with distinct taxpayer identification numbers, who are provisioned within Customer’s account through the Services. Monitored Taxpayers are the underlying taxpayers associated with a Monitored Client, and a Monitored Client may have one or more Monitored Taxpayers.

2.5 “Active Client Relationship”: One client engagement billed to one payer, together with every Monitored Taxpayer served under that engagement, regardless of the number of taxpayer identification numbers, entities, or tax years involved. A person or entity that engages Customer separately and is billed separately is a separate Active Client Relationship. Active Client Relationships are the sole basis on which Subscription Fees are calculated.

2.6 “Pricing Proposal”: The document identified in the signature block below and delivered with this Agreement, stating Customer’s included capacity, monthly Subscription Fee, billing contact, and any agreed commercial terms. The Pricing Proposal is incorporated into this Agreement by reference and is accepted by Customer’s execution of this Agreement. No separate signature to the Pricing Proposal is required.

2.7 “Monitored Data”: All IRS data, tax information, authorization forms (including Form 8821 or equivalent electronic authorization), transcripts, notices, documents, metadata, and any other information relating to Monitored Clients and/or Monitored Taxpayers that is submitted, uploaded, retrieved, or processed through the Services.

2.8 “Customer Data”: Business information, internal firm records, configuration settings, billing information, and other non-IRS data submitted by Customer or its Authorized Users.

2.9 “Client Consent”: A valid IRS authorization (Form 8821 or equivalent electronic authorization) executed by a Monitored Taxpayer authorizing Customer (and TaxRock as Customer’s technology provider) to retrieve IRS data for that Monitored Taxpayer.

2.10 “Documentation”: User guides, onboarding materials, and instructions provided by TaxRock.

3. Term and Termination

3.1 Effective Date: This Agreement becomes effective on the date of Customer’s signature.

3.2 Subscription Commencement: Customer’s subscription begins on the Effective Date. The first Subscription Fee is charged on the Effective Date and recurs on the same day of each month thereafter (the “Billing Date”), in accordance with Section 6. If a month does not contain the corresponding day, the Billing Date is the last day of that month.

3.3 Subscription Term: The subscription is month to month. There is no minimum term and no renewal date; it continues until cancelled. Either Party may cancel on at least thirty (30) days’ written notice, and the subscription ends at the close of the notice period. Customer authorizes TaxRock to charge the payment method on file through the end of the notice period. Customer may submit cancellation notice to support@taxrock.com.

3.4 Termination for Cause: Either Party may terminate this Agreement for material breach by written notice describing the breach. Termination takes effect at the end of the thirty (30) day cure period if the breach remains uncured. The date on which cancellation under Section 3.3 or termination under this Section takes effect is the “Effective Termination Date”.

3.5 Effect of Termination: Upon cancellation or termination:

a) Customer’s access to the Services ends on the Effective Termination Date.

b) TaxRock stops all transcript retrieval, monitoring, notifications, and related automated processing for Customer and its Monitored Clients and Monitored Taxpayers on the Effective Termination Date.

c) TaxRock does not provide data exports upon cancellation. IRS transcripts and related information can be obtained directly from the IRS at any time by Customer or its clients.

d) All unpaid fees accrued through the Effective Termination Date become immediately due and payable.

4.4 Restrictions: Customer may not sublicense, resell, assign, or transfer access to the Services without TaxRock’s prior written consent.

Adding capacity beyond that number is governed by Section 6.3. Deactivation or cancellation stops monitoring and related Services. Form 8821 and equivalent authorizations remain on file to allow for streamlined reactivation of Services, unless Customer requests their withdrawal.

6. Fees & Payment

6.1 Fees: The subscription fee for the Services is a flat monthly fee stated in the Pricing Proposal (the “Subscription Fee”). Pricing is scoped individually to each Customer based on the requirements of its practice.

6.2 Measurement: TaxRock’s system records are the authoritative source for the number of Active Client Relationships provisioned in Customer’s account. TaxRock does not audit Customer’s books, client list, or practice.

6.3 Included Capacity and Pricing Adjustments: The Subscription Fee includes up to the number of Active Client Relationships specified in the applicable Pricing Proposal, which Customer may provision at any time. Any revised Subscription Fee will take effect beginning with the first billing cycle following that acceptance and will not decrease based on reductions in Customer’s actual usage. This Section governs changes arising from Customer requiring capacity beyond the amount included in its Pricing Proposal; Section 6.7 governs changes initiated by TaxRock to the Subscription Fee for Customer’s existing capacity.

6.4 Pricing Structure: There are no platform fees, seat-based fees, or minimum commitments unless otherwise agreed in writing.

6.5 Billing & Payment: Subscription Fees are invoiced and charged monthly in advance on Customer’s Billing Date and are due upon receipt. Customer will maintain a valid payment method on file and authorizes TaxRock to charge that method for all undisputed amounts on each Billing Date. Fees are exclusive of sales, use, VAT, GST, and similar taxes, which are Customer’s responsibility where applicable. All amounts are in USD.

6.7 Price Changes: Subject to Section 6.3, which governs capacity increases, TaxRock may update the Subscription Fee for Customer’s existing capacity upon at least thirty (30) days’ written notice to Customer. Updated pricing takes effect at the start of the first billing cycle following the close of the notice period. Customer may terminate this Agreement without penalty by providing written notice of cancellation in accordance with Section 3 prior to the effective date of the price change.

6.8 No Refunds: All subscription fees are non-refundable except as expressly stated in this Agreement.
